Privacy & disclaimer

JotSprout is a local-first capture and planning tool. Your content stays on your device.

Local storage

JotSprout does not require an account. Notes, images, recordings, files, links and drafts are stored in the app's local shared container. The main app and share extension use the same local library.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or receive your notes, photos, recordings, files, social account labels or usage activity. The app has no advertising, tracking SDKs or online AI services.

Permissions

Photos, camera, microphone, speech recognition and notification permissions are requested only when the related feature is used. Image text recognition and supported voice transcription run on device.

Backups and deletion

Local saving is not an external backup. Export a complete backup from Settings and keep it outside the app. You choose where backup files are stored; JotSprout does not encrypt them. Deleted records remain in Recently Deleted before cleanup; clearing data removes local records and attachments.

External support

If you choose to submit a support request through GitHub, GitHub handles connection information under its privacy policy. Submitted issues are public. Do not include private notes, images or recordings.

Disclaimer

JotSprout helps capture ideas, organize drafts, schedule content and prepare for publication. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any social media platform.

Copying or sharing does not automatically publish content. Finish in the platform's official app, then confirm the status in JotSprout. Platform rules, suggested limits, OCR and voice transcription can change or contain errors. Verify original content.

Respect applicable laws, copyrights and third-party platform terms when using and publishing content.
